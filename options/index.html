How a project opens

Each link opens the main page with that style. Click any paper or project to see it open, try Next and Previous, then close it. Tell me the number you want.

  1. 1. Side panel
    The current one: a panel slides in from the right over the dimmed page.
  2. 2. Station grows
    The station you clicked grows into a full page in the line colour, which settles into the item.
  3. 3. Expands in place
    The item grows from where it sits into a centred reading panel, its title moving into place.
  4. 4. Next stop
    A full page slides in like pulling into a station, with a station sign and this line's stops.
  5. 5. Sheet from below
    A sheet rises from the bottom over the page; drag it down to close.
  6. 6. Split view
    The page moves left and a reading pane opens on the right, so the line stays in view.
